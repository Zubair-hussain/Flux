# Code of conduct

Flux is a friendly, welcoming project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else, agrees to:

- Be respectful and kind, and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, insults, sexual content, and personal attacks.
- Never publish anyone's private information without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who break these rules.

Report problems privately to **hello@zubairhussain.xyz** with the subject "Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.
